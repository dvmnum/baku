What it does

No block pages, no bans. Baku just makes doomscrolling dull.

Fading instead of blocking

The site stays open but slowly turns gray. You choose how gray and how fast.

Time counted honestly

Time runs only while you scroll, click or watch a video. A tab in the background doesn't count.

Own limit for any site

YouTube 20 minutes, TikTok 10, the rest share one limit.

Messages don't count

Instagram Direct, X messages, Reddit chat and other useful pages never cost time or go gray.

5 more minutes, if you really need it

You can extend, but after a short pause so it's not on autopilot. And only twice a day.

Private

No server, no accounts, no analytics. Everything stays in your browser, with access only to the sites you add.

Why "Baku"

The baku is a spirit from Japanese folklore that comes at night and eats bad dreams.

This one eats doomscrolling: it doesn't scold or forbid, it just quietly takes the color out of your feed when it's time for a break.
